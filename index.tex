\markboth{Presentación}{Presentación}

\includegraphics[width=1\textwidth,height=\textheight]{img/conclat_LIn_banner_1.png}

Este libro corresponde al repositorio de procesamiento y análisis de la
base de datos de la \href{https://www.conclat.org/encuesta/}{Encuesta
CONCLAT} aplicada en Chile, para ser presentado en
\href{https://www.ilpc.org.uk/}{ILPC 2025}. Y posteriormente en SASE.

La Encuesta Conclat se aplica en el marco del Proyecto CONCLAT, que
busca estudiar cómo se percibe el conflicto laboral y político en
Argentina y Chile. Particularmente, analiza cómo las personas de
distintas clases y con distinto estatus sindical (por ejemplo,
afiliados/as y no afiliados/as a sindicatos) perciben el conflicto
dentro y fuera del lugar de trabajo.

\href{https://www.conclat.org/}{CONCLAT} es el Proyecto FONDECYT Regular
(No 1230056) ``Clases sociales, movimientos sindicales y conflicto en
tiempos de crisis: un estudio comparativo de Argentina y Chile''.
Patrocinado por la \href{https://uchile.cl/}{Universidad de Chile} y el
Centro de Estudios de Conflicto y Cohesión Social
(\href{https://coes.cl/}{COES}). Tiene como propósito contribuir a la
investigación internacional sobre clases, trabajo y conflicto
produciendo datos cuantitativos originales y un instrumento de
investigación comparable a nivel internacional.

\textbf{Labor Union Power}: This index was calculated as the average

\textbf{Job control-autonomy}: This scale was constructed based on the
simple sum of four variables:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\item
  Please indicate to what extent you agree or disagree with the
  following statement about your job: I can work independently (1.
  Strongly agree\ldots{} 5. Strongly disagree);
\item
  Which of the following statements best describes how your work
  schedule is decided?

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    My employer decides when I start and finish work, and I cannot
    change it myself
  \item
    I can decide my start and finish time within certain limits
  \item
    I am completely free to decide when I start and finish work
  \end{itemize}
\item
  Which of the following statements best describes how your daily
  workday is organized?

  \begin{itemize}
  \item
    I am free to decide how my daily workday is organized
  \item
    I can decide how my workday is organized within certain limits
  \item
    I am not free to decide how my daily workday is organized
  \end{itemize}
\item
  How difficult would it be for you to take one or two hours off during
  working hours to attend to personal or family matters? (1. Not
  difficult at all\ldots{} 5. Very difficult).
\end{enumerate}

All these variables were recoded and transformed into a score from 0 to
4, where higher scores indicate greater perceived autonomy/control. The
items were then summed, and the final scale was transformed into a
0-to-100 score. The scale is unidimensional (Principal Axis Factor
Analysis: Eigenvalue = 1.45; 39\% of explained variance) and internally
consistent (Cronbach's Alpha = 0.70).

\hypertarget{dependent-variable}{%
\section{Dependent variable}\label{dependent-variable}}

We measured \emph{political participation} through the variable
\textbf{c11\_02}. \emph{I'm going to read you a list of voluntary
organizations. For each one, could you tell me whether you are an active
member, an inactive member, or not a member of that organization?
Political party}. The variable was coded ``Active'' = 1, and
``Inactive'' or ``Not a member'' = 0.

\hypertarget{independent-variables}{%
\section{Independent variables}\label{independent-variables}}

The first independent variable is \emph{workplace autonomy}, which
corresponds to the average of items \textbf{b06}. \emph{Now, thinking
about your current job, please tell me how much influence you have over
the following aspects:}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{b06\_01}. \emph{The methods or ways in which you carry out
  your tasks during the workday.}
\item
  \textbf{b06\_02}. \emph{The policies your company adopts regarding
  your working conditions.}
\item
  \textbf{b06\_03}. \emph{The time you start and finish your workday.}
\item
  \textbf{b06\_04}. \emph{The time you are allowed to take a break.}
\item
  \textbf{b06\_05}. \emph{The possibility of taking one or two hours off
  per week to attend to personal or family matters.}
\end{itemize}

Each item uses a response scale ranging from ``No influence'' = 1 to ``A
lot of influence'' = 5.

The second independent variable is \emph{union membership}. This was
measured through the variable \textbf{b21}. \emph{Are you affiliated
with a labor union (either company or inter-company) or a public
employees' association?}, coded as ``Union members'' = 1, and ``Not
member'' = 0.

\hypertarget{mediating-variables}{%
\section{Mediating variables}\label{mediating-variables}}

We use three mediating variables. The first is \emph{political
confidence}, which corresponds to the average of the following items
\textbf{c10}. \emph{How often do you do each of the following
activities?}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{c10\_01}. \emph{Talks about politics with friends.}
\item
  \textbf{c10\_01}. \emph{Tries to convince someone of your political
  views.}
\end{itemize}

Each item uses a response scale ranging from ``Never'' = 1 to ``Very
frequently'' = 5.

The second mediating variable is \emph{political skills}, which
corresponds to the average of the following items \textbf{c12}.
\emph{Imagine that in your community or neighborhood there is a problem
with the electricity supply (the power has been out for three days), and
the neighbors want to meet to solve the problem. Now I would like you to
answer the following:}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{c12\_01}. \emph{I would feel comfortable organizing and
  leading the meeting with my neighbors.}
\item
  \textbf{c12\_02}. \emph{I would feel comfortable leading some form of
  protest so that the authorities listen to our demands.}
\end{itemize}

Each item uses a response scale ranging from ``Strongly disagree'' = 1
to ``Strongly agree'' = 5.

The third mediating variable is \emph{identification with political
parties}, measured through the item \textbf{c09}. \emph{Which of the
following political parties best represents your interests, beliefs, and
values?}. The variable was coded as ``Left-wing parties'' = 1, which
includes Communist Party, Socialist Party, Frente Amplio, Partido
Humanista; and 0 = ``Does not identify with a party'' or ``Identifies
with right-wing parties''. The parties classified as left-wing are those
with a presence in the labor union sphere.

\hypertarget{controls}{%
\section{Controls}\label{controls}}

Our analyses included the following sociodemographic controls:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  gender (1 = ``Female'');
\item
  age (as a numeric variable);
\item
  education level, measured in four groups: 1. Lower (up to completed
  primary education), 2. Middle (up to completed secondary education),
  3. High (incomplete university education), 4. Upper level (completed
  university education or higher).
\item
  sector (1 = private sector; 0 = public sector);
\item
  political interest, measured by the item \textbf{c10\_01}. \emph{How
  often do you do each of the following activities? Talks about politics
  with friends} (1 = ``At least once'', 0 = ``Never'');
\item
  workplace size, measured in four groups: 1. 1 to 9 employees, 2. 10 to
  49 employees, 3. 50 to 199 employees, 4. 200 employees or more.
\end{itemize}
